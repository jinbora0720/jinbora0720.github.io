Research

Research Interests

  • Spatial statistics
  • Bayesian methods
  • Hierarchical models
  • Graphical models
  • Latent factor models
  • Environmental health

Much of the data in environmental health is spatial and multivariate, with complex dependence and missingness, which I often approach through the lenses of Bayesian methods and dependence learning. I build interpretable, scalable models; advance identifiable and robust inference for complex environmental systems; and translate results into open tools and actionable evidence for communities.
